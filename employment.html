Join Superior

Superior Ambulance Service has a longstanding presence in the Southern Tier. We welcome interest from qualified EMS providers who value clinical professionalism, patient care, and teamwork.

Opportunities may be available for New York State-certified EMTs and paramedics in full-time and part-time positions.

Benefits

  • Competitive wages
  • Flexible scheduling and 12-hour shifts
  • 401(k) with employer matching
  • Medical, dental, and vision coverage
  • Paid time off and holiday differential
  • In-house continuing education
  • Tuition reimbursement

Applicant Information

Applicants must hold appropriate New York State EMS certification and meet position-specific requirements.
