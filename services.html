WHAT WE DO

Services

Emergency medical response, patient transportation, and support for our community.

Dispatch

Superior operates its own dispatch center to help coordinate available ambulances, incoming requests, an unit assignments. Each dispatcher is trained and certified to answer Emergency and Non-Emergency calls by the International Academies of Emergency Dispatch (IAED). Our center receives approximately 13,000 calls annually, both through calling us directly and through the Broome County Office of Emergency Services (911).

Emergency Response

Once dispatched the focus of our team is the Emergency Medical Care of the patient(s) and ensuring that you get the closest available ambulance which is staffed by providers with the level of care consistent with your injury or illness.

Our ambulances and personnel are in a ready state 24 hours per day, 365 days per year. They are stationed or posted at our strategic locations throughout our community to ensure you get a safe and efficient response to your incident when you need it.

Interfacility Transportation

We provide medically appropriate ambulance transportation between healthcare facilities and other locations, including scheduled and urgent transfers.

Superior hosts providers certified in Basic and Advanced Life Support, as well as, Critical Care. Our diverse team enables transports for patients whose clinical needs require higher levels of prehospital care. The specific crew and resources are determined by the patient’s needs and operational availability.
